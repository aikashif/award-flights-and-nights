Futures payoff

★★★★★
Linear and symmetricP&L = (exit − entry) × lot × lots

Pricing: cost of carry

★★★★★
Simple:       F = S × (1 + r × t)
Continuous:   F = S × e^(r t)
With dividend: F = (S − PV(D)) × e^(r t)   or  F = S × e^((r − d) t)
Commodities:  F = S × e^((r + storage − convenience yield) t)

Ex: S 1,000, r 8%, 3 months → simple 1,020 · continuous 1,020.20
Fair value ignores views about future priceHigher r → higher FHigher dividend → lower F

Basis, contango, backwardation, convergence

★★★★★
TermCondition
Contango (normal)F > S, basis negative
BackwardationF < S, basis positive (dividend > interest)
ConvergenceF → S as expiry nears; basis = 0 at expiry
Basis strengthensS − F rises → good for long spot + short futures

Uses: hedge, speculate, arbitrage

★★★★★
SituationTrade
Hold shares, fear fallSell futures (short hedge)
Will buy later, fear riseBuy futures (long / anticipatory hedge)
Bullish with limited cashBuy futures (leverage)
Futures overpricedCash-and-carry: buy spot + sell futures
Futures underpricedReverse cash-and-carry: sell spot + buy futures
Arbitrage funds earn ≈ money-market rate

Beta hedging

★★★★★
Lots to hedge fully  = Portfolio value × β ÷ (Futures price × lot)
Change beta β₀ → β₁  = (β₁ − β₀) × Portfolio ÷ (Futures price × lot)   (−) sell · (+) buy

Ex: ₹1.5 cr, β 1.25, Nifty 25,000, lot 75 → 1.875 cr ÷ 18.75 L = 10 lots (sell)
Fully hedged portfolio earns ≈ risk-free rateHedge removes market risk, not stock-specific risk

Volume = contracts traded today; OI = contracts still open

★★★★★
