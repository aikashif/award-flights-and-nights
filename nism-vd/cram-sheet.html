Margins (clearing corporation)

Initial marginSPAN (portfolio worst-case loss, VaR based) + exposure margin. Collected upfront
MTMDaily profit/loss settled in cash, next day
Who paysFutures: both sides. Options: only seller pays margin; buyer pays premium
Trap: derivatives do not create or destroy wealth (zero-sum). Hedging removes downside and upside (futures). Options hedge removes downside only, at the cost of premium.

Ch15 Forwards & futures 15 marks Highest-mark chapter

Pricing: cost of carry

F = S × e^(r·t) continuous (workbook uses this) F = S × (1 + r·t) simple (exam approximation) F = (S − PV of dividend) × e^(r·t) with dividend Example: S = 1,000, r = 8%, t = 3 months (0.25) F ≈ 1,000 × e^0.02 = 1,020.20 (simple: 1,020)
TermMeaning
BasisSpot − Futures. Goes to 0 at expiry (convergence)
Contango (normal)Futures > Spot · basis negative
BackwardationFutures < Spot · basis positive (high dividend, shortage)
Cost of carryInterest − dividend/income
Fair valuePrice from cost-of-carry model

Arbitrage

SituationTradeName
Futures overpriced (F > fair)Buy spot + sell futuresCash-and-carry
Futures underpriced (F < fair)Sell spot + buy futuresReverse cash-and-carry
Arbitrage funds (MF) do cash-and-carry: buy shares, sell futures. Return ≈ money-market rate. Taxed as equity.

Hedging with index futures

No. of contracts = (Portfolio value × Beta) ÷ (Futures price × Lot size) Example: ₹1 crore portfolio, β 1.2, Nifty futures 25,000, lot 75 Contract value = 25,000 × 75 = ₹18.75 lakh Contracts = 1.2 crore ÷ 18.75 lakh = 6.4 → sell 6
You hold / fearHedge
Long shares, fear fallSell futures (short hedge)
Will buy later, fear riseBuy futures (long hedge)
Want to raise betaBuy index futures
Want to cut beta to 0Sell futures per formula above

Models and parity

Put-call parity (European): C + K·e^(−rt) = P + S Models: Black-Scholes (European, continuous), Binomial (step tree) Volatility in B-S: only input not directly observable → implied volatility
StyleIndia: index and stock options are European (exercise only on expiry)
SettlementIndex options: cash. Stock options: physical delivery
Buyer paysPremium only. No margin
Seller paysMargin (SPAN + exposure). Unlimited risk on short call
Traps: American = any time till expiry; European = only on expiry. Option buyer's loss is capped; seller's profit is capped. "Writer" = seller. Higher volatility helps buyers of both calls and puts.

Ch18 Interest rates & fixed income ≈6 marks Read before IRF

Rule 1: bond price and yield move opposite. Rates ↑ → price ↓.
Current yield = Annual coupon ÷ Market price YTM = IRR of all cash flows if held to maturity Modified duration (MD) = Macaulay duration ÷ (1 + y/n) % price change ≈ − MD × Δyield Example: MD 5, yield +0.50% → price ≈ −2.5% PVBP / DV01 = price change for 1 basis point (0.01%)
Duration is higher when…Duration is lower when…

Zero-coupon bond: Macaulay duration = maturity

Hedge with IRF

FearWhoTrade

Hedge ratio (contracts) = (MD_portfolio × Value_portfolio) ÷ (MD_futures × Value_per_futures) = PVBP of portfolio ÷ PVBP of one futures contract

Ch7 NAV, TER, pricing

NAV = (Market value of assets + accrued income − liabilities − expenses) ÷ Units outstanding Example: assets ₹110 cr, liabilities ₹10 cr, units 5 cr → NAV ₹20 Sale price = NAV (no entry load) Repurchase price = NAV × (1 − exit load) exit load goes back to the scheme
TER cap (top slab)Equity 2.25% · Debt 2.00% · Index fund / ETF 1.00% verify (SEBI revised TER rules 2025–26)
